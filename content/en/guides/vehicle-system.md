GTA6's vehicle system has been overhauled. The details below are **mostly from preview/demo reports**, with Rockstar-confirmed items called out separately.

## Fuel & charging

- Fuel cars need to refuel at gas stations; EVs plug into charging stations.
- In the shown build, refueling takes about **10 seconds**, designed to be "deep but not annoying."
- **Status**: widely reported, not yet confirmed in an official Rockstar statement.

## Car-theft tiers (luxury cars are harder)

- Regular cars can be broken into by smashing a window; expensive/luxury cars still need a **digital key cloner** and professional tools after breaking the window.
- These tools are **not available at the start** — you must unlock them via chop-shop / fence missions.

## The Waink app (theft scanner)

Scanning a target vehicle shows:

- Alarm type
- Required theft tools
- GPS tracker level
- Vehicle value
- Registration / legalization cost

## GPS trackers

- Some high-end stolen cars have trackers that **silently alert the police if you drive too slowly** — keep your speed up.
- Trackers come in tiers; completing more chop-shop missions unlocks removing higher-tier trackers.

## Garages & storage

- You can **buy garages** to store stolen cars and clear records/trackers.
- Official explanation: **you can't freely buy homes**, because that wouldn't fit Jason and Lucia's "on the run" story.
- Trunk capacity varies by vehicle: motorbikes carry almost nothing, while large trucks can haul multiple bags of cash/loot.

## Registration & legalization

- Waink shows the cost to **register a stolen car as your personal vehicle**.
- An unregistered stolen car can be hidden in a purchased garage until its tracker is removed and it's registered.
- **Pay 'n' Spray** returns: quick repair + repaint, which changes the vehicle description the police have on file.

## Impounding & recovery

- If your personal car is linked to a crime, police may stake it out; leave it too long and it gets **impounded** — pay to reclaim it or steal it back.
- Damaged vehicles can be restored for a fee via **Scooter Brothers**.

## Officially confirmed: nitrous

Nitrous boost has been officially confirmed.

## New driving model

- Blends GTA4's weightiness with GTA5's accessibility.
- More realistic damage: blowouts cause loss of control / rollovers, with Burnout-style crash cameras.
- Denser traffic and narrower streets make high-speed getaways harder.

> Except for nitrous and "no free home purchase," most details are from preview/demo reports and may change in the final game.
