GTA6 anchors globally at **$79.99 Standard / $99.99 Ultimate** in the US. Here are the official regional prices, plus the reference console prices to pair with them.

## Regional game prices (official)

| Region | Standard | Ultimate | Upgrade |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | $79.99 | $99.99 | $20 |
| United Kingdom | £69.99 | £89.99 | £20 |
| Eurozone | €79.99 | €99.99 | €20 |
| Canada | CA$109.99 | CA$139.99 | CA$30 |
| Australia | AU$129.95 | AU$159.95 | AU$30 |
| Japan | ¥9,800 | ¥12,280 | ¥2,480 |

> Platform and retail prices may differ slightly; the above is based on official announcements.

## Cheapest and priciest regions

Converted to USD, **South Korea, Japan and India** are among the cheapest places to buy GTA6; **Israel, Hungary and Switzerland** are the priciest. Cross-region buying comes with account, payment and language hurdles, so most players shouldn't bother.

## Reference console prices (US MSRP)

You'll need a next-gen console first. Common US pricing for the three:

| Console | Reference price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Xbox Series S | $299.99 | Cheapest, 1080p, no disc |
| PS5 (Digital) | $449.99 | No disc |
| PS5 (Disc) | $499.99 | With disc |
| Xbox Series X | $499.99 | Most powerful |

> Console prices fluctuate with sales; these aren't GTA6 pricing. For which console to choose, see the [console buying guide](/en/guides/gta6-console-buying-guide).

## The verdict

The game itself starts at $79.99, with the Ultimate adding $20 for exclusive content. For the console, pick Series S on a budget, or PS5/Series X for the full experience. Add it all up and the cheapest path is roughly $380 (Series S + Standard), the top end about $600 (PS5/Series X + Ultimate).

> Final prices are subject to the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Store and retailers.
