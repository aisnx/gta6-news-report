GTA6 costs **$79.99 (Standard)** or **$99.99 (Ultimate)** in the US. Here's the price by region, what you get, and whether it's worth it.

## Price by region

| Region | Standard | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|
| United States | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| Canada | $109.99 | $139.99 |
| Europe | €79.99 | €99.99 |

> Regional pricing varies by platform and retailer; the above is based on official announcements.

## Why $79.99?

- This is **$10 above** the last-gen AAA norm ($69.99), reflecting next-gen-only development.
- GTA6 has been in development for years and is Rockstar's biggest project to date.

## Is the Ultimate worth $20 more?

The Ultimate Edition adds exclusive vehicles/weapons/outfits, extra story-related content and exclusive stores/missions. Whether it's worth it depends on how much you value those extras — see our [editions comparison](/en/guides/gta6-editions).

## What the price includes / excludes

- **Includes**: base game, Vintage Vice City Pack (pre-order bonus), and (digital) one free month of GTA+.
- **Excludes**: early access (none exists), a physical disc (the physical edition is a download code).

## Bottom line

$79.99 is the entry price, and it's in line with (slightly above) current AAA pricing. For most players the Standard Edition is the best value; the Ultimate is only for those who want the exclusive extras.

> Final pricing is subject to the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Store and retailers.
