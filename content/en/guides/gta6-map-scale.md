Rockstar has been cagey about the exact map size, but the September previews put a number on it: **Vice City is reportedly around twice the size of Los Santos** — and that's before you count the rest of Leonida.

This is a **reported** estimate, not an official figure.

## What "2x Los Santos" means

Los Santos was GTA5's city. GTA6's single city being 2x that is a big jump — denser streets, more neighborhoods, more verticality.

## And there's a whole state around it

Vice City is only the core. Leonida also has the Keys, the wetlands, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga — the map isn't just a bigger city, it's a state with multiple distinct regions.

## How it compares

GTA5 was already huge. If the 2x figure is real and the surrounding regions are as big as described, GTA6 is comfortably Rockstar's largest world — and it's built entirely for current-gen hardware.

## What it means for you

Bigger doesn't always mean better, but here it likely means more travel variety: urban Vice City, swamp airboats, coastal highways, and a national park all in one map.
