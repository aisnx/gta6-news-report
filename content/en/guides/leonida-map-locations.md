GTA6 is set in the fictional state of **Leonida**, based on Florida, USA. Rockstar has confirmed **six major regions**, introduced one by one below.

## 1. Vice City

A modern reimagining of Miami and Leonida's core city. Known landmarks include:

- **Ocean Beach**: a coastal urban area
- **Little Cuba** (based on real-world Little Havana)
- **Tisha-Wocka flea market**
- **VC Port**

## 2. Leonida Keys

A tropical island chain based on the Florida Keys, full of beaches, bars and yacht culture, with dangerous surrounding waters.

## 3. Grassrivers

Swampland and mangroves based on the Everglades, patrolled by alligators; airboats are the main form of transport.

## 4. Port Gellhorn

A **declining former resort**: cheap motels, closed attractions and empty shopping malls — called Leonida's "forgotten coast," and a hotspot for dirt bikes and low-level crime.

## 5. Ambrosia

An industrial town built around the sugar industry, anchored by the **Allied Crystal sugar refinery** and the local motorcycle gang's territory.

## 6. Mount Kalaga National Park

Wilderness on the state's northern border offering hunting, fishing and off-road trails, dotted with "off-grid" (self-sufficient) communities.

## Other points of interest (seen in trailers, pending official confirmation)

- Ocean Drive
- Malibu Club
- Kelly County
- VCI Airport
- Brian's Boat Works

## Scale

Rockstar says the map is **larger than GTA V**; estimates of the exact size vary (some third parties put it at ~2× GTA V or more), pending official final data.
