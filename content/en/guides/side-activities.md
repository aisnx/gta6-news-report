GTA6's side activities and mini-games are the biggest yet. Below, grouped by category, are the activities **seen in trailers, gameplay demos or previews** (the final game may add or remove some).

## Racing

- Street races
- Off-road motorbike / mud racing
- Track / circuit racing
- Off-road events
- Daytona / Sebring-style racetrack

## Water & outdoors

- Diving (reefs, shipwrecks, richer underwater environments)
- Fishing
- Swimming
- Kayaking / canoeing
- Jet skis
- Airboats
- Hunting
- Waterskiing / sunbathing

## Fitness & combat

- Gym workouts / weightlifting (with a bench-press mini-game)
- Working out with your partner (affects body shape)
- Cage fighting / MMA
- Backyard wrestling
- Boxing

## Ball games & sports

- Basketball
- Pool / billiards
- Mini-golf

## Air & extreme

- Skydiving / parachuting
- Base jumping

## Nightlife

- Bars
- Nightclubs
- Strip clubs
- Dancing

## Other mini-games & activities

- Shooting range
- Gambling
- Electric scooters
- Petting / observing / feeding animals
- Scrolling the in-game social feed
- Spying on NPCs
- Watching TV
- Eating and drinking
- Collecting vehicles

> This list is compiled from trailers, gameplay demos and preview reports; the final content is subject to Rockstar and the released game.
