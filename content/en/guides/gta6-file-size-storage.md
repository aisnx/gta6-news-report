Rockstar hasn't announced GTA6's install size, and anyone quoting an exact number is guessing. But you can plan around what we already know.

## What's certain

- GTA6 is digital in practice: the physical version is a download code, so the whole game lands on your SSD.
- GTA5 has grown past 100GB after years of GTA Online updates, and GTA6 is a much bigger world.

## What to expect

A realistic guess: **150GB minimum, and 200GB+ wouldn't surprise me.** Don't plan your storage around the low end.

Here's what each console gives you to work with:

| Console | Total SSD | Usable space |
|---|---|---|
| PS5 | 825GB | ~667GB |
| Xbox Series X | 1TB | ~802GB |
| Xbox Series S | 512GB | ~364GB |

The squeeze is the **Series S**. A 200GB GTA6 would eat over half its usable space.

## Do you need an SSD?

- **PS5 with a couple of games installed** — probably yes. 667GB disappears fast.
- **Xbox Series X** — you're fine unless you hoard.
- **Xbox Series S** — GTA6 alone gets tight, and expansion here is more limited.

On PS5, a compatible **NVMe SSD** is the cheapest way to stop deleting games every time you want something new.
