GTA6 stars **Jason Duval × Lucia Caminos**, repeatedly described by Rockstar and the press as a "**Bonnie and Clyde**"-style crime couple. Here's a look at the story based on **official trailers and confirmed information**; all speculation is clearly labeled and never mixed with leaks.

## Confirmed story foundations (grounded)

- **Lucia**: recently released from Leonida Penitentiary, locked up for "fighting to protect her family," with a social worker named Stefanie; after release she resolves to "make every move smart."
- **Jason**: a former soldier turned drug dealer, the calmer half of the duo.
- **Setting**: the state of Leonida (based on Florida), with a modern Miami-style Vice City.
- **Signature line** (trailer 2): Lucia — *"The only thing that matters is who you know and what you got."*

## Story-direction theories (reasoned, not spoilers)

### 1. "On the run" is the core frame

Rockstar explained that you can't freely buy homes because it wouldn't fit Jason and Lucia's "on the run" story — suggesting the main campaign is a **sustained flight**: starting from Lucia's release and reunion with Jason, the two survive through crime while evading the police (six stars return) and bigger forces.

### 2. The "Bonnie and Clyde" fate of the ending (high-risk speculation)

The real Bonnie and Clyde died in an ambush. GTA has always satirized Hollywood endings; combined with the dual-protagonist and six-star setup, **a tragic ending is possible**. But Rockstar could equally offer "go straight vs. stay criminal" multiple endings, echoing GTA5. **This is pure speculation** — the final version rules.

### 3. A trust crisis between the two

Lucia is impulsive and sharp, Jason is calm and rational — complementary but also a setup for **disagreement or even betrayal**. Each protagonist's "unique abilities" may point to different choices on the same events.
