With *GTA6* around the corner, the question players ask most is: "What's actually changed from GTA5?" This article compares the two item by item to set expectations.

## 1. Release timeline

| Milestone | GTA 5 | GTA 6 |
|---|---|---|
| First revealed | 2011-11 (first trailer) | 2023-12 (first trailer) |
| Launch | 2013-09-17 (PS3 / Xbox 360) | 2026-11-19 (PS5 / Xbox Series X\|S) |
| PC | 2015-04 (~19 months later) | Unannounced (expected 2027+) |
| Next-gen upgrade | 2022 (PS5 / Xbox Series X\|S) | — (next-gen at launch) |

## 2. Platform strategy

- **GTA5**: launched on last-gen consoles, then PS4/Xbox One in 2014, PC in 2015 and next-gen in 2022 — spanning three generations.
- **GTA6**: launches **next-gen only** (PS5 / XSX\|S), with the PC version unannounced — the biggest cadence difference between the two.

## 3. Protagonists

| Dimension | GTA 5 | GTA 6 |
|---|---|---|
| Number | 3 (Michael / Franklin / Trevor) | 2 (Jason / Lucia) |
| Switching | Three switchable at any time | Two switchable, emphasizing "partner" cooperation |
| Female protagonist | None | Yes (Lucia, the first in the modern mainline) |
| Relationship | No fixed romantic bond | A "Bonnie and Clyde"-style crime couple |

## 4. Map & setting

| Dimension | GTA 5 | GTA 6 |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | San Andreas (California) | Leonida (Florida) |
| Core city | Los Santos | Vice City |
| Scale | ~80 km² (incl. ocean; estimates vary) | Officially larger than GTA5; third parties estimate ~2× or more |
| Composition | Los Santos + Blaine County | Six regions (urban / keys / wetlands / port city / industrial town / national park) |

## 5. Wanted system

- **GTA5**: five-star wanted levels; police spawn out of nowhere and lock onto the player's location.
- **GTA6**: **six-star wanted levels return** (first since GTA4), with a reworked police AI — someone must witness or trigger an alarm to report you, and police track you by clues (clothing / face / plate / vehicle), which you must clear one by one.

## 6. Vehicles

- **GTA5**: no fuel concept; stealing a car is basically grab-and-go.
- **GTA6**: fuel/charging, tiered luxury-car theft, GPS trackers, garage registration, nitro boost and more (mostly from demo reports).

## 7. Online mode

- **GTA5**: GTA Online launched **~two weeks after** the game, monetized via Shark Cards.
- **GTA6**: online mode **unannounced**; launch focuses on single-player, which is confirmed to have no microtransactions.

## 8. Price

- **GTA5** launch: $59.99 (last-gen standard price).
- **GTA6**: Standard $79.99 / Ultimate $99.99.

## Summary

GTA6 is bolder than GTA5 in platform strategy (next-gen only at launch), protagonist structure (a duo with a female lead), and the wanted/vehicle systems (more hardcore), with a larger map and more content. For veterans, the shift from a "three-protagonist sandbox" to a "dual crime couple" is the biggest experience change.

> This is a text-based comparison based on public facts and officially confirmed information; details of unreleased GTA6 features are subject to the final version.
