GTA6 ships in two editions: **Standard ($79.99)** and **Ultimate ($99.99)**. No Collector's Edition has been announced. Here's the side-by-side.

## Edition comparison

| | Standard $79.99 | Ultimate $99.99 |
|---|---|---|
| Base game | ✅ | ✅ |
| Limited-time pre-order bonus | ✅ | ✅ |
| Extra vehicles/weapons/outfits | — | ✅ |
| Extra story-related content | — | ✅ |
| Exclusive stores & missions | — | ✅ |

## Pre-order bonus (both editions)

- **Vintage Vice City Pack**: 1980s-style outfits and vehicles, for pre-ordering or purchasing before 2026-11-20.
- **One free month of GTA+**: included with digital pre-orders.

## Which to choose?

- **Standard** is enough if you only care about the story and base game.
- **Ultimate** is worth the extra $20 if you want the exclusive vehicles/weapons/outfits, extra story-related content and exclusive stores/missions.
- Standard owners can **upgrade to Ultimate later** by paying the difference.

## What's not included

- **No Collector's Edition** has been announced.
- **No early access** in either edition.
- **Physical edition is a download code**, not a disc.

## Bottom line

If you just want the campaign, Standard is fine; if you value extra content and collectibles, go Ultimate.
