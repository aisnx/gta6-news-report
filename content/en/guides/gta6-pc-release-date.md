GTA6 launches 2026-11-19 on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only**. The **PC version has no release date** — Rockstar hasn't announced one. Here's what we know and what history suggests.

## What's official

- **PC is not a launch platform** — the 11-19 release is console-only.
- Rockstar has **not confirmed** a PC date, pricing or system requirements.

## What history suggests

Rockstar consistently releases its big titles on consoles first, then PC later:

| Title | Console | PC | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA V | Sep 2013 | Apr 2015 | ~19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | Oct 2018 | Nov 2019 | ~13 months |

GTA6's PC port is expected to follow the same pattern — **likely 2027 or later**, though this is unconfirmed.

## What the rumors say

Multiple reports point to a **2027** PC release (unconfirmed). We track the latest in our [PC release rumors](/en/news/gta6-pc-release-rumors).

## What this means for PC players

- You'll **wait at least a year** (likely more) after the console launch.
- Don't buy a console just for GTA6 unless you don't want to wait — but also don't hold your breath for a near-term PC date.
- When PC is announced, we'll also get official [system requirements](/en/guides/gta6-system-requirements).

## Bottom line

There's no GTA6 PC release date yet. Based on Rockstar's history, expect **2027 or later**. We'll update this page the moment an official date is confirmed.

> The PC release date is unannounced; the 2027 estimate is speculation based on Rockstar's history, not an official announcement.
