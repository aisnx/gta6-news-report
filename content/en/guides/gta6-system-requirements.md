GTA6 launches 2026-11-19 on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only**. The PC version has **not been announced**, which means **there are no official PC system requirements yet**. Here's what we know, what to infer, and what's still unconfirmed.

## What we know (official)

- **PC is not a launch platform** — GTA6 ships console-first.
- **No official PC specs** have been published.
- Rockstar has **not confirmed** a PC release date (rumors point to 2027+).

## What we can infer from Red Dead Redemption 2

RDR2 (Rockstar's last PC port, 2019) required roughly:

| Tier | RDR2 (2019) |
|---|---|
| Minimum | GTX 770 / RX 280 |
| Recommended | GTX 1060 / RX 480 |

GTA6 is a far more demanding, next-gen-only title, so expect requirements to sit **substantially higher**.

## Our estimates (unconfirmed — treat as speculation)

| Tier | Speculation (do not buy hardware on this) |
|---|---|
| Minimum (1080p/30) | RTX 2060 / RX 6600, 16 GB RAM, SSD |
| Recommended (1440p/60) | RTX 3070 / RX 6800, 16 GB RAM, SSD |
| High (4K/60+) | RTX 4070 Ti / RX 7900 XT, 32 GB RAM, NVMe SSD |

> These are our own educated guesses based on console specs and Rockstar's history — **not** official numbers. Don't upgrade your PC until Rockstar publishes real specs.

## What we can be confident about

- **SSD is effectively required** — next-gen titles are built around fast storage.
- **16 GB RAM** is the sensible floor for 2026+ AAA games.
- Expect **~150 GB+** of storage (GTA5 plus updates already exceeds 100 GB).

## Bottom line

Wait for Rockstar's official PC announcement and specs before buying or upgrading hardware. We'll update this page the moment real requirements are published.
