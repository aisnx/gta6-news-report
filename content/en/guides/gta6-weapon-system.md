GTA5 let you pull a rocket launcher out of thin air mid-chase. GTA6 reportedly doesn't. That single change — a real inventory — ripples through everything else.

These are **reported** details from the September previews, not official Rockstar announcements.

## The 4-gun limit

You can carry **four weapons**: two long guns, one handgun, one melee. That's your loadout.

Everything else has to live somewhere — your **vehicle trunk** or a **hideout**. So before a heist, you actually think about what you're bringing, the way you'd pack for a trip.

## Auto-aim is reportedly gone

The assist that's carried console players for two decades is, by multiple reports, **removed**. You aim manually, the way you do in a PC shooter.

This is the one that'll split the room hardest. Some players are already complaining; a lot of others think it's overdue.

## The robbery loop

With a real inventory comes a real economy:

- Almost **every store** is rob-able — convenience stores, jewelry shops, banks.
- You carry the haul to a **fence** and sell it.
- Better gear means better scores, which loops back into your loadout.

It's a tighter loop than GTA5's "rob a store for pocket change." The whole thing is built to make you care about what you carry and what you risk.

## What it means

If all of this ships as reported, GTA6 plays slower and more deliberately than GTA5. You plan the job, pack the right four guns, drive a getaway car whose trunk actually matters.
