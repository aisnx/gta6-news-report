Partnering with Lucia, **Jason Duval** is GTA6's other playable protagonist. Here's what we know about him — and the supporting cast we can actually name.

## Jason Duval

- **Background**: a former soldier turned drug dealer after leaving the military.
- **Role**: forms a "Bonnie and Clyde"-style crime duo with Lucia Caminos; switchable at any time during missions and in the open world.
- **Personality**: compared to Lucia's impulsiveness, Jason is calmer and the more rational half of the duo.

> Rockstar has revealed less background detail on Jason than on Lucia; this site will keep updating as official info is released.
