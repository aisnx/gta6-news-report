GTA6 launches on 2026-11-19. Digital or physical? This article breaks down the differences.

## One key premise

GTA6's **physical edition is actually a "download code"**, not a disc — so both editions ultimately require an internet download of the game. The traditional "physical saves you a download" advantage does not apply to GTA6.

## Core differences

| Dimension | Digital | Physical |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $79.99 / $99.99 | Same |
| Medium | No physical item, direct download | Download code (no disc) |
| Pre-order bonus | Vintage Vice City Pack | Same |
| GTA+ month | Included with digital pre-order | Varies by retailer |
| Availability | Playable at launch after pre-load | Wait for shipping/pickup |
| Collectability | None | Packaging box (for collectors) |

## Which to choose?

- **Want to play at launch, minimal hassle** → Digital (pre-load opens 11-12, playable at launch)
- **Want the box for collecting, or gifting** → Physical (note it's a download code)
- **Game content is identical** — no exclusive-content differences

## Pre-load timing

Digital pre-load opens on **2026-11-12**. The physical edition also needs an internet download after arrival, and can't pre-load.

## Summary

If you only care about playing as early as possible, go digital; if you value the box, go physical. Game content, price and pre-order bonuses are identical.
