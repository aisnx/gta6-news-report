GTA6's wanted system has been overhauled — police no longer "spawn out of nowhere." Here's the split: **officially confirmed principles** vs **leaked-build / preview details**.

## Officially confirmed principle

Rockstar co-head Rob Nelson confirmed: "The wanted system requires **someone to witness the crime, or an alarm to be triggered**, before it's reported." — meaning if nobody sees you, the crime may not immediately summon the police.

## Six stars return (per leaked builds, not an official announcement)

- Six-star wanted levels were absent after GTA4 (GTA5 had only five); leaked footage shows six stars returning in GTA6.
- The leaker claims the footage is "100% real," but Take-Two keeps issuing DMCA takedowns; the exact mechanics are subject to the final release.

## Reworked police AI & pursuit

- **Realistic response times**: police arrive based on distance from the precinct, not instant spawning.
- **Persistent identification**: police record your appearance, clothing and plate; changing clothes/cars only works if done unseen.
- **Tactical cordon**: they use dogs, helicopters and searchlights to set a search perimeter rather than beelining to your hiding spot.
- **Pre-escalation warning**: police first shout through a loudspeaker as a warning.

## Four star-icon states

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Solid white star | Police have a direct line of sight and are actively chasing |
| Flashing white star | Line of sight lost; police are searching your last known location |
| Red star | Police lost the trail, but you're still in the searched "hot zone" |
| Hollow star | Crime reported, but you're not yet locked as a suspect |

At six stars, the "hot zone" can cover the entire map.

## Evidence icons (intel the police hold)

Shown below the wanted stars, these must be cleared one by one:

- **Hanger**: your outfit is identified → fully change clothes
- **Person**: face/appearance recorded → change hairstyle or alter your look
- **Camera**: caught on CCTV → avoid camera-dense areas
- **Car icon**: vehicle tracked → abandon the car
- **Two-person icon**: Jason and Lucia seen together → split up
- **Weapon icon** (mentioned in some preview reports, pending final confirmation): weapon identified

## Six-star breakdown

| Stars | Response |
|---|---|
| 1 star | Local patrol: verbal warning, minor fine, routine stop |
| 2 stars | Vice City PD: active pursuit, PIT maneuver, basic roadblocks |
| 3 stars | State police + air support: spike strips, spotlight tracking, cordons |
| 4 stars | SWAT: armored vehicles, door breaching, snipers |
| 5 stars | Federal agency (FIB): highway lockdown, heavy firepower |
| 6 stars | Military: city-wide lockdown, heavy weapons, aggressive interception |

## Escape tips

- **Break line of sight**: duck into alleys, underground areas or the wilderness.
- **Switch cars unseen**: steal a new car when nobody's watching.
- **Wear a mask before the crime**: avoid facial identification.
- **Change clothes + hairstyle**: invalidate clothing/identity evidence.
- **Split up**: clear the "seen together" clue.
- **Blend into crowds**: merge into dynamic crowds and indoor spaces.

> Much of this detail comes from leaked early builds and preview reports, not official Rockstar announcements; mechanics and thresholds may change in the final release.
