Bottom line: for GTA6, a **controller and an SSD are the two things actually worth spending on** — the controller decides how the game feels, the SSD decides whether you can even install it. Headphones and a charging dock are nice-to-haves.

## One thing first: accessories don't cross platforms

- GTA6 launches 2026-11-19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and accessories follow your console.
- **The PS5 controller has adaptive triggers + haptic feedback**, which the Xbox controller can't match.
- The physical version is a download code (no disc), so the whole game lands on your SSD — **storage matters more than you think**.

## Which controller

**On PS5, the DualSense is the answer.** Adaptive triggers give the throttle resistance while driving and trigger tension while shooting; haptics make chases feel physical. Sony also announced two GTA6-themed DualSense colors ($85, out with the game on 11-19) — same controller, new shell, so if you're buying anyway, just get that one.

**On Xbox, the controller uses standard rumble**, no trigger feedback. The upside is it's cheap and tough, and your existing Xbox pad probably still works fine — no need to upgrade for GTA6.

**Third-party controllers** (fight sticks, pro pads) usually drop the console-specific features. Unless you need something specific like back paddles, don't buy one for GTA6.

## The SSD: the part that'll actually bite you

Rockstar hasn't announced GTA6's install size. A realistic guess is **150GB minimum, 200GB+ not surprising** (see the [GTA6 file size guide](/en/guides/gta6-file-size-storage)). Against usable space:

| Console | Usable space |
|---|---|
| PS5 | ~667GB |
| Xbox Series X | ~802GB |
| Xbox Series S | ~364GB |

- **PS5 with 2–3 AAA games installed** → you'll likely want a **NVMe SSD** (make sure it's PCIe 4.0 rated).
- **Series S** → GTA6 alone could eat half of it, and expansion is limited to Microsoft's own cards.
- **Series X** → fine unless you hoard games.

## Headphones and charging dock: optional

- **Headphones**: get one if you play late or want proper immersion; the controller speaker is fine to start.
- **Charging dock**: DualSense battery life is average, and a dock keeps a full controller ready — a small, cheap buy.

## What to buy by budget

- **One thing** → get the **SSD** first (not deleting games is the most practical win).
- **Room for two** → SSD + a GTA6-themed DualSense.
- **Xbox players** → skip a new controller; put the money into an expansion card or Game Pass.
