## 2. Rumored radio station list (leaked, unconfirmed)

The station names below mostly come from leaked footage and radio menus; **Rockstar has not officially confirmed them**:

- CircoLoco Records Radio
- Dirty South Classics
- Honey FM
- Back Country Radio
- Emotion 98.3
- ON.U / Radio ON-U
- Stockyard FM
- Symphony FM

> Of these, **V-Rock** and **Emotion 98.3** are classic stations from *GTA: Vice City* with strong demand for a return — especially since Jason wears a V-Rock t-shirt in promo art.
