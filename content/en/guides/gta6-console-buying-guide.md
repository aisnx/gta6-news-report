GTA6 launches 2026-11-19 on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only**; the PC version is unannounced (rumors point to 2027+). If you're buying a console for GTA6, this article helps you decide.

## One fact first

- **Launch platforms**: PS5, Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S
- **PC**: unannounced, not launching alongside
- **Exclusive content**: Rockstar has **not announced** any platform-exclusive content — the launch experience is essentially identical across platforms

## PS5 vs Xbox Series X|S

| Dimension | PS5 | Xbox Series X | Xbox Series S |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positioning | Flagship | Flagship | Entry next-gen |
| Visuals / perf | High | High | Lower (targets 1080p) |
| Price | Higher | On par with PS5 | Lowest |
| Controller | Adaptive triggers | Standard | Standard |

## Recommendations

- **Best visuals & controller feel** → **PS5** (adaptive triggers could add a unique feel to driving and gunplay in GTA6)
- **Already in the Xbox ecosystem / on a budget** → **Xbox Series S** (cheapest way to play GTA6)
- **Already own any next-gen console** → **don't switch**, just wait for launch

## Beyond budget

- Do you already have a game library or friends on a platform? **Ecosystem lock-in** often matters more than a single game.
- The physical edition is a download code (no disc), so physical-vs-digital doesn't affect your console choice.

## A note for PC players

If you only have a PC and don't want a console: the PC date is unannounced, with rumors pointing to **2027** (unconfirmed). We track it in [GTA6 PC release rumors](/en/news/gta6-pc-release-rumors).

> Console comparisons are based on public specs and general knowledge; GTA6's actual per-platform performance is subject to post-launch testing. Refer to official Rockstar announcements.
