Lucia Caminos is one of GTA6's two playable protagonists, and the **first playable female protagonist** in the modern GTA mainline. She forms a "Bonnie and Clyde"-style crime duo with Jason Duval.

## Background & family

- **Liberty City roots**: grew up in the GTA version of New York, with a Hispanic background.
- **Her mother's influence**: her mother has dreamed of "making a good life" since the Liberty City days.
- **Fight training**: her father "taught her to fight from the moment she could walk," giving her a strong close-combat foundation.

## Prison & release

- **Why she was locked up**: "fighting to protect her family" sent her to **Leonida Penitentiary**; the exact charge is unconfirmed, only hinted as family-related.
- **Prison social worker**: a correctional social worker named **Stefanie**, seen in the first trailer.
- **Release**: Rockstar officially described it as "sheer luck"; some sources say she was paroled in the 2020s.

## Personality & motives

- **Motivation**: she wants the "good life" her mother dreamed of, but refuses to settle for "half-baked fantasies" — she "takes her fate into her own hands."
- **Personality**: more **impulsive** than Jason, but also smart and calculating; after release she resolves that "every move from here on has to be smart" — widely read as turning to more precise crime rather than going straight.
- **Signature line** (trailer 2): *"The only thing that matters is who you know and what you got."*

## Exclusive weapons & vehicles (per Ultimate Edition and official material)

**Weapons**

- Engraved Compact SMG (close-range main weapon)
- Hawk & Little Morgan Revolver (shared with Jason)

**Vehicles**

- The Red Tulip (personal sedan)
- '55 Vapid Stanier
- '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy
- '95 Grotti Cheetah
- Shitzu Squalo speedboat
