Bottom line: there's no GTA6 mobile version. It launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and the only way to play on a phone is cloud gaming or streaming — which Rockstar hasn't confirmed for GTA6.

## What's confirmed

- GTA6 launches 2026-11-19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC yet, and definitely no mobile.
- Rockstar has never announced a GTA6 mobile, Switch, or handheld version.
- The physical version is a download code, and the game is hundreds of GB — a phone couldn't hold it anyway.

## Can you play on phone? One narrow path

You can't install or play it natively. The only theoretical option is streaming a console to your phone:

- **PS Remote Play** streams your own PS5 to your phone — the PS5 has to be on, and you have to own GTA6.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming** runs the game on Microsoft's servers with your phone as the screen. But whether GTA6 ever enters the cloud library, or when, hasn't been announced.

Neither is a "mobile version." Both are remote play, with worse picture and latency, and both require a console or a subscription.

## Don't fall for these

- **"GTA6 mobile APK"** — fake. Any link claiming a GTA6 mobile download right now is a scam or malware.
- **"GTA6 mobile pre-registration"** — fake. Rockstar hasn't announced any mobile port.
- **"Switch version"** — also unannounced. Don't hold your breath.

## Want to play GTA on a phone?

Rockstar has ported older games like GTA: San Andreas to phones before, but those are remasters of old titles, not GTA6. For the new one, get a PS5 or Xbox.

## The short version

- **Want to play GTA6 at launch** → get a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S (see [which console to pick](/en/guides/gta6-console-buying-guide)).
- **Only have a phone** → there's no official way right now. Don't spend a cent on "GTA6 on phone."
