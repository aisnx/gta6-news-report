GTA6's in-game phone includes a built-in **social media system**, deeply tied to **dual-protagonist switching**. This article covers what's known and what's still unconfirmed.

## In-game social media

Accessible from the character's phone:

- **Scrolling viral videos**
- **Following in-game influencers / streamers**
- **Discovering world events and hidden side missions via the feed**

The many short social-media-style clips in the first trailer are seen as a teaser of this mechanic.

## Dual-protagonist switching

- You can **switch between Jason and Lucia** during the story, including **real-time switching** within missions.
- The mechanic resembles GTA5's character switching, but with a stronger emphasis on **"partner" cooperative missions**.
- Each protagonist reportedly has **unique abilities** that affect missions and the crime economy.

## Still unconfirmed

- Whether free switching in the open world works exactly like GTA5, or is limited to specific missions/story moments, has not been clarified by Rockstar.
- Some details come from retail listings and leaks (e.g. an Amazon Brazil product page); Rockstar has not publicly confirmed everything.

> Dual playable protagonists are officially confirmed, but the exact form of free switching is subject to the final release.
