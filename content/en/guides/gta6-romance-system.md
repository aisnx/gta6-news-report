Since the first trailer, the internet's big question has been: do Jason and Lucia get together? The September previews finally give an answer, and it's the best possible one for a Rockstar game — **it's up to you.**

Details below are **reported**, not officially confirmed.

## The three ways it can go

- **Romantic** — you push them toward being a couple.
- **Platonic** — partners in crime, nothing more.
- **Ignored** — you never touch it, and they keep working together anyway.

The key word is *optional*. Rockstar isn't railroading a love story; it's handing you a relationship you can shape or ignore.

## No scripted date missions

Instead of "go here, trigger a cutscene, relationship +1," you reportedly build outings yourself — the zoo, kayaking, a walk on the beach. The relationship comes out of how you spend time, not a checklist.

## It's a state, not a bar

No numeric meter, no "Lucia liked that." It's a relationship *state* that shifts over time, and reports say it can touch parts of the story. One reassuring detail: even if you mostly play one character, the relationship reportedly won't just break or turn the other into a nag.

## What's still rumor

The big unconfirmed bit: whether it goes as far as a full intimate relationship. Some outlets reported it, Rockstar hasn't. Until there's a trailer or a statement, treat that as fan speculation.

## Why it matters

It's a small thing that says a lot about the game. GTA6 isn't forcing a Bonnie-and-Clyde love story — it's handing you the two of them and letting the relationship be whatever you make of it.

Are you pairing them up, or keeping it strictly business?
