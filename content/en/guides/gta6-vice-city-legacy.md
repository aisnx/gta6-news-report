*GTA: Vice City* (2002) is a classic for countless players. Now GTA6's stage returns to the same city — a modernized Miami-style Vice City within the larger state of Leonida. This article maps the ties between the two.

## Why "returning to Vice City" is a big deal

- The GTA series rotates between cities, but **Vice City** is one of the most fondly remembered settings.
- GTA6 is the first time the city is reimagined in the **HD era** (previously HD-era games only covered Liberty City and Los Santos) — the long-awaited "Miami return."

## Confirmed / trailer-seen ties

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Vice City** | The core city, modern Miami style |
| **Ocean Beach** | A coastal urban area, nodding to the original's same-named district |
| **Little Cuba** | Echoes the original's Little Havana, with a slightly changed name |
| **Malibu Club** | The original's iconic nightclub, seen in trailers (pending official confirmation) |
| **Ocean Drive** | Miami's famous street, seen in trailers |

## Radio station returns (the big tribute)

GTA6's soundtrack article already lists officially confirmed songs. In the rumored radio lineup, **V-Rock** and **Emotion 98.3** are two iconic stations from the original *Vice City* — and Jason wears a **V-Rock t-shirt** in promo art, widely read as a deliberate nod to longtime fans.

## The differences: old vs new

| Dimension | Vice City (2002) | GTA6 (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Era | 1980s | Modern (near-future) |
| Map | A single city | The state of Leonida (six regions) |
| Protagonist | Tommy Vercetti (single) | Jason × Lucia (dual, first female lead) |
| Tech | PS2 era | Next-gen (PS5 / XSX\|S) |
