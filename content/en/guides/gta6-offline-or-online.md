Bottom line: GTA6's single-player will almost certainly be playable offline — GTA5 always has been — but you'll need internet for the initial download and updates. Online mode isn't there at launch, so always-on internet isn't a thing yet.

## What's confirmed

- GTA6 launches 2026-11-19 with **single-player only**; GTA Online 2 has no date.
- Single-player has **no microtransactions** — Rockstar confirmed that.
- The digital version downloads over the internet, and the physical version is a download code, so you need internet to activate and download it too.

## Can you play single-player offline?

Almost certainly yes. Rockstar's pattern hasn't changed:

- **GTA5**: once installed, story mode is playable offline.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2**: same — single-player works offline.

GTA6 will almost certainly follow: install and update, then play single-player offline. Strictly speaking, though, Rockstar hasn't separately confirmed GTA6's offline specifics.

## When do you need internet?

- **Initial download/install**: the digital version downloads the whole game, and the physical version needs internet to activate the code.
- **Patches**: the day-one patch and later updates all need a connection.
- **Online mode** (when it arrives): that's always-online by nature.

## What this means for you

- **Bad internet isn't a blocker**: once installed and updated, single-player works offline.
- **Don't buy "offline" deals**: cheap "offline activation" offers are usually shared accounts or piracy — account-ban risk is on you.
