Straight answer: **nobody outside Rockstar knows GTA6's frame rate yet, because Rockstar hasn't said.** No 60fps confirmation, no "performance mode" announcement, nothing. But you can narrow it down a lot from the hardware, Rockstar's history, and what they've actually shown.

## What's confirmed (the short version)

- Rockstar has **not** announced a 30fps or 60fps target, nor a Fidelity/Performance mode split.
- Trailer 2 was explicitly captured **on a base PlayStation 5** — Rockstar said so in the trailer's own description.
- The PS5 and Xbox Series X can output **up to 4K at 120Hz**, but that's a display spec, not a GTA6 promise.

That's the whole confirmed list. Everything below this line is a realistic read of it, clearly labeled.

## What Rockstar's track record says

Rockstar has a very consistent pattern:

- **RDR2** shipped at **30fps** on PS4/Xbox One and, notably, **never got a 60fps patch** on PS5/Xbox Series X.
- **GTA5** ran 30fps last-gen, then got **60fps** years later through GTA5: Expanded & Enhanced.
- **GTA6** is bigger than both — a map roughly twice the size of Los Santos, with dense NPC crowds.

The pattern: Rockstar ships **30fps first** and treats 60fps as a later nicety, not a launch promise.

## The realistic expectation (not confirmed)

Here's the read I'd bet on:

- **A 30fps "Fidelity" mode is near-certain.** Max resolution, max effects — the Rockstar default.
- **A 60fps "Performance" mode is possible but unconfirmed.** The hardware can do it at a resolution cost; whether Rockstar bothered is the open question.
- **120fps is not happening** for an open-world game at this scale, whatever the box says.

If 60fps is a dealbreaker for you, don't pre-order on faith — wait for the launch-day breakdown.

## What to buy to be ready either way

Since nobody can tell you the frame rate, prepare for the worst case and you're covered:

- **A 120Hz display with HDMI 2.1.** Even if the game caps at 30 or 60, a good panel keeps the option open and helps every other game.
- **A console with SSD headroom.** GTA6's install size isn't confirmed, but expect 150GB+ — see our [storage guide](/en/guides/gta6-file-size-storage). Xbox Series S is tightest.
- **Don't chase "60fps" marketing now.** Any site promising a confirmed 60fps GTA6 is guessing — including the ones selling pre-orders off it.
