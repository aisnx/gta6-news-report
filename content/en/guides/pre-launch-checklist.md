GTA6's official launch (2026-11-19) is getting close. This checklist helps you sort out everything before launch.

## 1. Choose your platform

- Launch is **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S** only; the PC version is unannounced.
- If you only have a PC, you'll need to wait for an official announcement (rumors point to 2027, unconfirmed).

## 2. Choose your edition

| Edition | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $79.99 | Players who just want the base game |
| Ultimate | $99.99 | Players who want vehicles/weapons/outfits + extra story-related content |

## 3. Pre-order & pre-load

- The pre-order bonus **Vintage Vice City Pack** is available until 2026-11-20.
- Digital pre-load opens on **2026-11-12**; the physical edition is a download code (no disc).
- Digital pre-orders include one free month of **GTA+**.

## 4. Storage & network

- No specific storage size has been announced; leave plenty of free SSD space.
- You'll need an internet connection on day one to download/activate — check your network and storage ahead of time.

## 5. Day-one plan

- On launch day (11-19), dive into the **single-player campaign** first.
- The online mode (GTA Online 2) will **not** be available at launch — don't wait for it.
- Follow this site: from launch day we'll update main-story, easter egg, collectible and ending guides.

> Specific details are subject to official Rockstar Games announcements.
