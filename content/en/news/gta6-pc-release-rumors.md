*GTA6* launches only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and **the PC date has still not been officially announced**. A recent rumor has reignited the PC conversation.

## The rumor

- Leaker **DetectiveSeeds** claims to have contacted **90+ former Rockstar employees** via LinkedIn; only 3 replied, and their accounts were consistent.
- The rumor points to a **February 2027** PC release — about 3 months after the console version (2026-11-19).
- The reasoning: Take-Two wants to ship the PC version **before the fiscal year ends (2027-03-31)** to boost the annual report.
- The rumor also stresses that "the timeline may shift, nothing is set in stone."

## Historical comparison

| Game | Console → PC gap |
|---|---|
| GTA V | ~19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | ~13 months |

If February 2027 is accurate, it would sharply shorten Rockstar's usual PC gap.

## The counterargument (why it's doubtful)

- Rockstar has historically **deliberately delayed the PC version** to encourage "double-dipping" (buying on console first, then PC).
- Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick previously said PC launches are **intentionally delayed** to prioritize consoles, while also admitting PC has become "increasingly important."

## Conclusion

**A February 2027 PC release is a single-source rumor** from a handful of unnamed former employees; neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it. This site will keep tracking official announcements.

> Always refer to official Rockstar Games announcements, and do not make purchase decisions based on this.
