Tomorrow is the biggest GTA6 news day since the September preview: Game Informer drops digital issue **#382 on September 29** with a **14-page GTA6 cover story**, **12 new screenshots** and exclusive developer interviews. Print copies follow later. If you want something more than the usual rumor mill, this is it.

## What's in the cover story

- A **14-page cover feature** on GTA6, built on interviews Rockstar gave Game Informer directly.
- **12 new screenshots** — the largest single image drop since the August extended look.
- Digital issue goes live **September 29**; print follows.

## Rockstar is now saying "on time" out loud

Rockstar North co-studio lead **Rob Nelson** told Japanese magazine Famitsu that GTA6 **"will be released on time."** It's the closest thing to a hard reassurance we've had, and it lines up with Take-Two repeating the November 19 date in earnings calls.

The math backs it up: **September 30 marks 50 days out.** We're now closer to launch than we are to the August extended look.

## The store pages quietly updated

Two store-side changes worth knowing:

- Preorders now carry a hard **cutoff of November 19, 23:59:59 local time**, per the updated store listings.
- Retail press reports say **Xbox is worried** that GTA6 preorders are heavily concentrated on **PS5**. That part is unconfirmed, but it's the kind of platform split worth watching before you commit to a box.

## And a $400 "Collector's Box" — merch, not a game edition

Reports now describe a **$400 Collector's Box** that ships merchandise but **no game code**. Note the distinction: this is not a Collector's *Edition*. Our [editions guide](/en/guides/gta6-editions) still holds that nothing beyond Standard and Ultimate has been announced. The box is physical merch only — the kind of thing that sells out on its own.

## What I'd actually watch for

The cover story is the thing to set a reminder for. Twelve screenshots and a 14-page feature are exactly what Rockstar does when it's confident — and a lead saying "on time" out loud is a stronger signal than a dozen anonymous rumor posts.

Will you read the Game Informer story tomorrow, or wait for the screenshots to get reposted everywhere?
