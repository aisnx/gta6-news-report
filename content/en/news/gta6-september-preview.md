Rockstar didn't drop a new trailer. What it did was fly a roomful of creators to Rockstar North, and the resulting previews just spilled a pile of GTA6 systems we'd only guessed at. The three that matter most: a **Criminal Profile** system that tracks what kind of criminal you are, an **optional romance** between Jason and Lucia, and a hard **4-weapon inventory** that ends the GTA habit of carrying an arsenal in your pockets.

Everything below comes from those creator reports and press coverage. None of it is a formal Rockstar announcement, so treat the specifics as "reported" until the game ships.

## The Criminal Profile system

This is the headline. Instead of an honor meter, GTA6 tracks **what kind of criminal you are**:

- **Professional**, **Aggressive**, **Violent**, **Psycho** — four states, and your behavior moves you between them.
- A fifth, unnamed state: if you go far enough off the rails, the icon **shatters and locks permanently**. The game stops grading you.
- Jason and Lucia each have their **own** profile, so you can play the two leads completely differently.
- Unlike RDR2's Honor, it's not a good/evil scale — it's a "what's your style" scale. Self-defense doesn't count against you, for example.

## The romance is optional (and that's the point)

The big fan question — do Jason and Lucia get together — has an answer: **only if you want them to.**

- Romance them, keep them as platonic crime partners, or ignore the whole thing. The duo keeps working together regardless.
- No scripted "date missions." You build outings yourself — zoo, kayaking, a walk on the beach.
- It's a state, not a numeric bar, and it can touch the story.

> One corner of this is still rumor: whether it goes as far as an explicit relationship. Rockstar hasn't confirmed that part.

## The weapon system got harder

- You carry **four weapons**: two long guns, one handgun, one melee. That's it.
- Everything else lives in a **vehicle trunk or a hideout**, so loadouts actually matter now.
- **Auto-aim is reportedly gone** — you aim for real.

## And a bunch of smaller stuff

- **Robberies are everywhere**: nearly every store is rob-able — convenience stores, jewelry shops, banks — and you sell the haul to fences.
- **Sprint is changing**: Rockstar is reportedly killing "tap X to sprint"; you click the left stick instead. Some fans are already annoyed.
- **Cops got sharper**: new wanted stars and evidence icons tracking your weapon, identity, clothes, the couple, and CCTV.
- **Scale**: Vice City is reportedly around **twice the size of Los Santos**, with a lot more map outside the city.
- **Length**: one report put the main story at around **80 hours** without touching side content.

## What I'd actually bet on

The Criminal Profile and the 4-gun limit are the two that change how GTA6 *feels*. If both land, it's a tighter, more deliberate game than GTA5 — less "pocket arsenal," more "what kind of crook are you."
