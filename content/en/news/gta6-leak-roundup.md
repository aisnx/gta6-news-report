## Claim 1: Leonida's map is ~2.5x the size of GTA V

- **What the leak says**: a recent clip shows a much larger, denser Leonida map than earlier estimates.
- **Our read**: Plausible. Rockstar has confirmed six major regions (see our [map reference](/en/guides/leonida-map-locations)), so a large map is expected.
- **Verdict**: Likely true — but the exact multiplier is unconfirmed.

## Claim 2: The main story has roughly 80–100 missions

- **What the leak says**: an alleged internal milestone lists around 80–100 main missions, more than GTA V's 69.
- **Our read**: Uncertain. Rockstar hasn't confirmed mission count.
- **Verdict**: Unconfirmed — treat as rumor.

## Claim 3: First-person mode is in at launch

- **What the leak says**: leaked UI footage allegedly shows a first-person toggle.
- **Our read**: Uncertain. First-person was added to GTA V in a post-launch update, not at launch.
- **Verdict**: Unconfirmed, leans dubious.

## Claim 4: The day-one patch will be ~50–60 GB

- **What the leak says**: a "download size" leak claims a large day-one patch.
- **Our read**: Uncertain. No storage size has been announced.
- **Verdict**: Unconfirmed — don't plan storage around it yet.

## Claim 5: GTA Online 2 arrives a few months after launch

- **What the leak says**: online mode is allegedly targeting early 2027.
- **Our read**: Plausible but unconfirmed — Rockstar has said launch is single-player only.
- **Verdict**: Unconfirmed. Track it in our [GTA Online 2 rumor](/en/news/gta-online-2-rumors) article.

## The bottom line

Everything above is **unconfirmed**. Rockstar has not commented on any of these leaks, and some "leak videos" circulating right now are almost certainly fake or spliced from old footage. For a clean split between confirmed fact and rumor, check our [verify page](/en/verify).
