Sony confirmed it: GTA6 is getting its own DualSense controllers. Two colors, $85 each, pre-orders open September 10, and they drop November 19 with the game.

## What Sony announced

At State of Play on September 3, Sony showed two limited-edition GTA6 DualSense controllers:

- **Black** and **white** versions
- **$85 / €85 / £75**
- Pre-orders start **September 10**
- Release **November 19**

Under the GTA6 shell they're standard DualSense controllers, so you still get adaptive triggers and haptic feedback.

## Why the DualSense matters for GTA6

If you're playing GTA6 on PS5, the controller is already a reason to pick that console. The adaptive triggers add real resistance to the throttle and trigger in driving and shooting, and the haptics make chases feel physical. A GTA6-themed version is cosmetic, but if you're buying a controller anyway, the design is free.

## Should you buy one?

- **Buying a DualSense anyway** — get the themed version. Same $85, no downside.
- **Already have a working controller** — it's a looks upgrade. Skip it unless you collect.
- **On Xbox** — this is PS5-only. Series X|S uses standard controllers.
