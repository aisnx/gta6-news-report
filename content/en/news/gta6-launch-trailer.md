On October 15, Rockstar released the **GTA6 Launch Trailer**, a roughly 90-second video captured entirely on a standard PS5. With launch on November 19 under five weeks away, this is the last major official trailer before release.

## What the trailer shows

- **A "simple job" gone wrong**: the official synopsis has Jason and Lucia lining up an easy score that spirals out of control, dragging them into a criminal conspiracy across the state of Leonida.
- **Jason's military past**: the trailer opens with Jason working at a marina, mentioning he left the US Army and "didn't leave on the best of terms", before taking a call from Lucia — their relationship runs through the whole video.
- **Lucia wears an ankle monitor**: a hint that early in the story she's still under judicial supervision.
- **Action everywhere**: a vault heist, street chases, a low-flying biplane stunt over the beach, and a helicopter crashing into a high-rise.

## Soundtrack: Justice on the score

- French electronic duo **Justice** is confirmed as composer of GTA6's original score.
- The trailer debuts the single **"Flower in a Hurricane"**, featuring **Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard**, from the official album *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* (~34 tracks).

## Key dates

- **November 12**: preload begins for pre-orders.
- **November 19**: launch on PS5 / Xbox Series X|S (PC still unannounced; expected 2027+).
