Rockstar recently gave clear answers via official channels and media interviews to two questions players care about: **whether development used generative AI**, and **whether the single-player mode has microtransactions**.

## The official word

- **Generative AI**: when Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson was asked directly whether the studio used generative AI, he answered a clear **"No."** The studio stressed that all game content is made by human developers; traditional AI is only used to drive routine NPC, traffic and police behavior.
- **Microtransactions**: officially confirmed — GTA6's **single-player campaign contains no microtransactions (in-game purchases)**.

## What this means for players

- The single-player story has no paywall; content is delivered complete.
- The business model for the online mode (GTA Online 2) has not been announced — this site will keep tracking it.
