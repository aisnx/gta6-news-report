Two rumors just got killed by Rockstar itself: GTA6's development did **not** use generative AI, and the single-player story has **zero microtransactions**.

## The AI answer

Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson was asked point-blank whether the studio used generative AI. His answer was a flat **"No."** All content is made by human developers. The "AI" in the game is the traditional kind that's been running NPCs, traffic and police since forever.

## The microtransactions answer

Also confirmed: the single-player campaign has **no in-game purchases**. The story ships complete, no paywall.

## What's still open

The online mode is a different story — its business model hasn't been announced. If microtransactions ever show up, that's where they'd land. Worth keeping an eye on.
