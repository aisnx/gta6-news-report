For two decades, GTA players have mashed a face button to run. According to the September previews, that ends in GTA6 — sprint moves to a **left-stick click**, and the change is already splitting the fanbase.

This is **reported**, not an official Rockstar announcement.

## What's changing

- The old "tap X / A repeatedly to sprint" is reportedly gone.
- You click the left stick instead, the way a lot of modern games already do it.

## Why people care

It sounds trivial, but "tap X to sprint" is muscle memory for a generation of players. Reactions are split:

- **Relieved**: the old method wrecked thumbs and made the button do double duty.
- **Disappointed**: some players genuinely liked the rhythm of it, and losing it feels like losing a bit of GTA's DNA.

## What it actually means

It's a small control change with a big signal: Rockstar is willing to modernize GTA's feel, even at the cost of nostalgia. If tap-to-sprint is on the table, other old habits probably are too.
